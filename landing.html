What you get

No subscriptions, no sign-in, no onboarding maze.

As many windows as you want

Every widget is a standalone 2D panel. Spawn, close and rearrange them freely in Horizon.

Always live

Clocks tick, countdowns run, pomodoros switch, alarms ring in the panel — while it is open.

Per-window configuration

Each window keeps its own settings and one of six RLDS accent colors, stored locally.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Your data never leaves the headset.

Featherweight

Under 8 MB. A pure 2D app — no VR scene, no hand tracking, nothing to wear off.

Light & dark

Follows the system theme, or force either. The same UI Set look as Horizon itself.

How it works

Three steps, ten seconds.

01

Pick a type

Browse the catalog: Time, Productivity or Info. 21 types, from world clocks to a water tracker.

02

Spawn a window

One tap opens the widget in its own adjacent panel — the gallery stays behind, ready for more.

03

Make it yours

Open the window settings: time zones, goals, feeds, lists — and pick an accent color.

Your data stays on your headset

Widget instances, lists and settings are stored locally. The only network access is the optional Weather and News widgets, which query the public services you configure yourself — no API keys, no tracking, no accounts. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
