Widgets Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-27

This privacy policy applies to the Widgets app (hereby referred to as "Application") for Meta Quest devices that was created by Savva (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. It does not collect, sell, or share any personal information. All of your data is stored locally on your device only and never transmitted to the Service Provider or any third party:

  • Your widget instances — the windows you have spawned (clock, date, countdown, quote, note, battery, alarm, weather, pomodoro, tracker, calculator, water, news, tasks, etc.)
  • Your per-widget configuration (alarm times, tasks, water intake goals, pomodoro durations, notes, countdown targets, etc.)
  • Your app settings (theme, accent colors, and other preferences)

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your VR device, does not access your contacts, camera, or microphone, and does not use advertising identifiers.

Weather and News widgets

Two of the widget types fetch live data from public, keyless web services, and only while that widget window is open and you have configured it:

  • Weather requests forecast data from Open-Meteo. When you set a city or place for the widget, its name (or the coordinates looked up for it) is sent to Open-Meteo to retrieve the forecast. You are not required to use this widget; it does not work and sends nothing if you never configure a location.
  • News loads news items from RSS/Atom feeds whose URL you enter yourself. The feed URL is sent to the feed's host to retrieve its public content.

No other data, no identifiers, and no information about your device or other widgets are sent to these services. Which feeds or places you use is stored only on your device (see above). The terms and privacy practices of Open-Meteo and of the feed hosts apply to their respective services.

Optional Meta telemetry

The Application uses the Meta Spatial SDK. Meta may offer optional, opt-in diagnostics/telemetry for Meta SDKs; this is governed by Meta's own privacy policy and terms (see Meta Privacy Policy). Telemetry is disabled unless you opt in through the system or SDK prompts, and opting in is not required to use the Application. The Service Provider does not receive, access, or review any telemetry data.

Permissions

The Application requests only the Internet permission, which the Weather and News widgets need to fetch their data as described above. It requests no other runtime permissions.

Third Party Access

The Service Provider does not share, sell, or transmit any of your information to third parties. The only outbound requests the Application makes are the weather and feed requests you explicitly configure, and the optional Meta SDK telemetry described above (handled entirely by Meta, and only if you opt in).

The Service Provider may disclose information:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;
  • when they believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your VR device or via the VR application marketplace or network. Uninstalling the Application permanently removes your widget instances, configurations, and settings from the device. You can also avoid any network use entirely by not configuring the Weather or News widgets. If you opted in to Meta SDK telemetry, you can disable it in the Meta system settings; the Service Provider does not control or receive that data.

Data Retention Policy

All Application data (widget instances, configurations, settings) is stored only on your device and is retained only as long as the Application is installed. The Service Provider has no server-side copies of your data and cannot access your data. If you delete the Application, your local data is deleted with it.

Children

The Service Provider does not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. Because the Application does not collect any personal information, the Service Provider has no such data to delete; if you believe a child has used the Application, please contact the Service Provider at oisurka@gmail.com so that they can take any necessary actions.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Because the Application stores your data only on your device and transmits nothing off-device except the weather and feed requests you explicitly configure (and optional, opt-in Meta SDK telemetry handled by Meta), there is no Service Provider-side storage to secure. The Service Provider provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards for any information they process or maintain.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes to the Privacy Policy by updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.
