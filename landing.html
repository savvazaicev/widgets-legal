Widgets

Phone widgets, reimagined for Meta Quest. Spawn live 2D windows — clocks, notes, tasks, weather and more — and pin them where you want them in Horizon.

Free · No ads · No accounts · Works offline

What it does

Horizon has no home-screen widgets — so Widgets turns them into 2D windows. Pick a widget type from the catalog, spawn one or several windows, and each window shows its widget live: a ticking clock, a running countdown, an active pomodoro, fresh weather, your to-do list. All windows update in real time and stay in sync with each other.

  • Catalog of widget types — pick what you need, spawn as many windows as you like
  • Multi-window — each widget is its own Horizon panel; open several at once and arrange them freely
  • Per-widget configuration — every window is configured independently (time zones, alarm lists, task lists, goals, feeds…)
  • Live — clocks tick, countdowns tick down, alarms ring in the panel, trackers count
  • Private — everything is stored locally on your headset; no accounts, no ads, no analytics
  • Lightweight — under 8 MB, fast to install, 2D-only (no VR scene, no tracking)

Privacy

Your widgets, settings and data never leave your device. The only network access is the optional Weather and News widgets, which query the public services you configure yourself. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
